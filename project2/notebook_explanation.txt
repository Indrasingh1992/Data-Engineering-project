CELL de81873b — OUTLIER DETECTION (IQR METHOD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CODE:
    Q1, Q3 = df[col].quantile(0.25), df[col].quantile(0.75)
    IQR = Q3 - Q1

EXPLANATION:
    .quantile(0.25) = 25th percentile (Q1) — bottom quarter cutoff.
    .quantile(0.75) = 75th percentile (Q3) — top quarter cutoff.
    IQR (Interquartile Range) = the spread of the middle 50% of data.
    This is the standard Tukey method for outlier detection.

OUTPUT:
    Computed internally per column. No direct print here.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CODE:
    outliers = df[(df[col] < Q1 - 1.5*IQR) | (df[col] > Q3 + 1.5*IQR)]

EXPLANATION:
    Standard Tukey fence formula:
      Lower fence = Q1 - 1.5 × IQR
      Upper fence = Q3 + 1.5 × IQR
    Any value outside these fences is an outlier.
    | is the OR operator — flags values below lower OR above upper fence.

OUTPUT:
    QUANTITYORDERED:  8 outliers  (Q1=27.00, Q3=43.00, IQR=16.00)
    SALES:           81 outliers  (Q1=$2203.43, Q3=$4508.00, IQR=$2304.57)
    PRICEEACH:        0 outliers  (Q1=$68.86, Q3=$100.00, IQR=$31.14)
